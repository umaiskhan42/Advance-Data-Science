# Results — Zero-Shot Microfauna Anomaly Detection with Frozen DINOv3 on MD-AS-2025

Generated by `scripts/02_patchcore.py` and `scripts/03_visualize.py`.
Backbone is **frozen** throughout — no gradient updates, no task labels used to
build the model. Healthy taxa = normal; stressed taxa (Arcellinida, Nematoda)
are never seen during memory-bank construction.

## Experimental setup

| | |
|---|---|
| Backbone | DINOv3 ViT-S/16 (`vit_small_patch16_dinov3.lvd1689m`), frozen |
| Method | PatchCore — 3×3-aggregated patch embeddings, greedy-coreset memory bank (5000 vectors), max nearest-neighbour distance |
| Train (memory bank) | 1,509 healthy images (Hypotrichida, Digononta, Monogononta) |
| Test | 2,154 images — 647 healthy + 1,507 stressed (Arcellinida 800, Nematoda 707) |
| Metric | image-level AUROC / AP (threshold-free) + operating-point F1 |

## H1 — DINOv3 beats random-init and CNN baselines (CONFIRMED)

| Backbone | Method | AUROC | AP | best F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DINOv3 (frozen)** | PatchCore | **0.791** | **0.899** | **0.761** |
| DINOv3 (frozen) | CLS-kNN (image-level) | 0.781 | 0.894 | — |
| ResNet-50 (ImageNet) | PatchCore | 0.591 | 0.783 | 0.565 |
| Random-init ViT | PatchCore | 0.526 | 0.736 | 0.442 |

DINOv3 (0.79) ≫ ResNet-50 (0.59) ≫ random ViT (0.53 ≈ chance). The random-init
ViT sitting at chance shows the signal comes from **pretraining**, not the
architecture; the ImageNet CNN does better than chance but is well short of the
self-supervised features. → `results/csv/metrics_summary.csv`,
`results/figures/auroc_comparison.png`, `roc_curves.png`.

### Per-taxon (per-failure-mode) AUROC

| Comparison | DINOv3 | ResNet-50 | Random ViT |
|---|---|---|---|
| healthy vs **Arcellinida** | **0.788** | 0.522 | 0.543 |
| healthy vs **Nematoda** | **0.793** | 0.669 | 0.507 |

DINOv3 is balanced across both stressed taxa; the baselines are erratic
(ResNet catches Nematoda partly but misses the testate amoeba Arcellinida). →
`results/csv/per_taxon_auroc.csv`, `results/figures/per_taxon_auroc.png`.

## H3 — Clean separation in feature space

t-SNE / UMAP of DINOv3 CLS embeddings (`results/tsne/`) show the stressed taxa
occupying distinct regions from the healthy cluster, consistent with the AUROC.
The score distributions (`results/figures/score_distributions.png`) show
stressed taxa shifted to higher anomaly scores, with Digononta the hardest
healthy taxon (largest overlap with the stressed range).

## H2 — Biologically meaningful attention & localisation

`results/attention/` — DINOv3 last-block CLS self-attention overlays. `results/
heatmaps/` — PatchCore patch-distance heatmaps localising the anomalous object
within the frame (the elongated nematode body / the discoid testate shell).

## Operating points (DINOv3)

| Operating point | Threshold | Precision | Recall | Specificity | F1 | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Healthy 95th-pct (unsupervised) | 0.653 | 0.946 | 0.381 | 0.949 | 0.543 | 0.552 |
| Youden-J | 0.608 | 0.866 | 0.678 | 0.756 | 0.761 | 0.701 |

→ `results/csv/threshold_metrics.csv`. The 95th-percentile rule is the
deployable unsupervised choice (high precision/specificity, conservative
recall); Youden-J shows the achievable balance.

## Failure cases

`results/failure_cases/` (montages + `results/csv/failure_cases.csv`): worst
false positives (healthy frames scored anomalous — typically debris-heavy or
atypical-pose ciliates) and false negatives (stressed frames scored normal —
small/early-stage nematodes resembling elongated healthy forms).

## H4 — Blueprint for WWTP monitoring (an extension of EMSEL's lineage)

A frozen, label-free vision foundation model reaches ~0.79 AUROC distinguishing
stress-indicating microfauna with **zero task supervision and zero fine-tuning**
— a low-cost, label-efficient front-end for activated-sludge / aeration
monitoring.

Framed against the lab's work, this is not a new paradigm but the **image-domain
continuation of EMSEL's normal-prototype anomaly detection**: the same "memorise
the normal, flag the deviation" recipe used for WWTP sensors
(deep variational residual autoencoder, *Chemosphere* 2022) and IAQ data
(memory-augmented autoencoder with learned *normal prototypes*, *KJChE* 2025),
now applied to microscopy images via a frozen DINOv3 + PatchCore memory bank.
PatchCore is precisely the visual analogue of the lab's 2025 normal-prototype
memory. See [`Literature/CLOSEST_LAB_WORK.md`](Literature/CLOSEST_LAB_WORK.md).
